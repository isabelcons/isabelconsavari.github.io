About

Isabel Consavari is a Digital Media Art student working with photography, collage, video, web-based projects, and image manipulation. Her work often explores memory, family, intimacy, grief, and the emotional meaning held inside personal images and archives.

Through layering, distortion, glitch, and nonlinear navigation, she is interested in how images can shift between documentation and feeling. Her projects use digital tools to transform personal photographs into visual spaces that feel fragmented, intimate, and emotionally charged.
